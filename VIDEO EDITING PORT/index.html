About the Editor

THE MIND
BEHIND THE MOTION.

I am an independent video editor and visual storyteller passionate about creating powerful visual experiences.

I combine technical precision, creative thinking, and an understanding of rhythm to transform raw footage into compelling stories. Every cut, every transition, every frame is a decision — and I treat each one as if it matters, because it does.

My approach is collaborative and editorial: I listen first, then build. Whether it's a 15-second ad or a feature-length narrative, the goal is the same — to make the audience feel something they can't quite name.
